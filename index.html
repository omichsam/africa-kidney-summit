Priority focus areas

Four pillars driving Africa's kidney health agenda.

The summit programme is built around the four priority areas that will shape the next decade of kidney care on the continent.

Pillar 01 Clinical Nephrology Prevention, diagnosis and management of chronic kidney disease across African health systems.
Pillar 02 Transplant Medicine Donor matching, immunosuppression protocols and long term graft survival outcomes.
Pillar 03 Health Systems & Financing Sustainable funding models and national kidney care strategies for the continent.
Pillar 04 Screening & Early Detection Community screening programmes and point of care diagnostics across Africa.
