Priority focus areas

Four pillars driving Africa's kidney health agenda.

The summit programme is built around the four priority areas that will shape the next decade of kidney care on the continent.

Pillar 01 Clinical Nephrology Prevention, diagnosis and management of chronic kidney disease across African health systems.
Pillar 02 Transplant Medicine Donor matching, immunosuppression protocols and long-term graft survival outcomes.
Pillar 03 Health Systems & Financing Sustainable funding models and national kidney care strategies for the continent.
Pillar 04 Screening & Early Detection Community screening programmes and point-of-care diagnostics across Africa.

Who Should Attend

Every voice in Africa's kidney health ecosystem.

The summit is open to everyone working to improve kidney health across the continent — from ministries and clinicians to students, advocates and industry partners. All are welcome at the same table.

Government & Policy Leaders Ministries of health, regulators, public health agencies and parliamentary health committees. Policy action
Nephrologists & Clinicians Consultants, physicians, surgeons and paediatric specialists working in renal care. Clinical practice
Nurses & Allied Health Dialysis, transplant and renal nursing teams, dietitians and technicians. Frontline care
Researchers & Academia Universities, research institutes and health data networks driving kidney research. Evidence & data
Students & Trainees Medical students, residents, fellows and early-career health professionals. Next generation
Civil Society & NGOs Patient groups, kidney foundations, advocates and community health organisations. Community voice
Industry & Private Sector Device manufacturers, diagnostics companies, pharmaceutical firms and care providers. Innovation
Media & Press Accredited journalists, health reporters and communications professionals covering health. Public awareness
